Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

Big Wow Games is an independent developer of games on the Roblox platform. Our games run on Roblox, and Roblox Corporation is responsible for player accounts, sign-in and payments under the Roblox Privacy Policy.

What our games store

  • Game progress tied to your Roblox user ID: in-game currency, unlocked items, upgrades, collections and daily rewards. It is stored in Roblox DataStores so you keep your progress.
  • Anonymous gameplay analytics provided by Roblox (for example, which tutorial steps players finish and how long sessions last). We use them only to make our games better.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal information from players, and we do not sell or share any data.

Our social media accounts

We publish gameplay videos on our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. We may use the official YouTube and TikTok APIs to read statistics and comments on our own videos and to reply to comments. This data is used only internally to decide which videos and game features players like. We do not access other users' accounts and do not use this data for advertising.

Children

Many Roblox players are children. Our games follow Roblox Community Standards and age guidelines and do not ask players for personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions can be sent through our YouTube channel.
